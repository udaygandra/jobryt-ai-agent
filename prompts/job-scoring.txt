EVIDENCE-FIRST EVALUATION
Before assigning scores, identify the evidence in the posting and compare it to the candidate context.

Score each pillar from 0-100:
- title_fit_score: judge by the actual duties/function, not title wording alone.
  90-100 = target role or a senior/junior variation of the target role.
  70-89 = different title but substantially the same function.
  40-69 = adjacent function with meaningful overlap.
  0-39 = different profession/function.
- skills_fit_score: compare the posting's requirements to the candidate's demonstrated skills. Required skills matter more than nice-to-haves. Do not treat familiar/explicitly qualified skills as equivalent to demonstrated core skills.
- seniority_fit_score: compare the posting's required years/level and title seniority to the candidate's years and target roles. A gap of 3+ years either way scores below 50.
  * If the posting is for Lead, Principal, Staff, Manager, Director, VP, or Head, but the candidate does NOT have those in their target roles, score seniority below 40.
  * If the candidate targets Executive/Management roles, entry-level/internship roles must score below 30.
- domain_fit_score: direct or reasonably transferable match with the candidate's demonstrated domains and project experience.

FIELD RULES
- `key_matched_skills`: only skills/technologies that are explicitly present in BOTH the candidate context and the job posting.
- `missing_skills`: only job requirements explicitly stated in the posting that are not supported by the candidate context. Do not invent missing requirements.
- `disqualification_reasons`: hard blockers only, such as:
  * Missing required citizenship, security clearance, or work authorization
  * Location mismatch with no workable remote/hybrid option
  * Seniority mismatch (e.g. Lead/Management role for an IC candidate, or entry-level role for an Executive)
  * Duplicate or spam posting
  * Empty array if no hard blockers exist
- `company_tier`: choose only when the posting/company context supports it: FAANG_TOP_PRODUCT, TOP_ENTERPRISE, STRONG_STARTUP, MID_TIER, LOW_QUALITY, or UNKNOWN.
- `work_type`: Remote, Hybrid, Onsite, or Unknown.
- `compensation_insight`: quote the stated salary/range exactly, or "Not stated". Never estimate.
- `why_this_fits`: maximum 2 sentences.
- `notes_concerns`: maximum 2 sentences.
- `reasoning`: maximum 3 sentences.

DO NOT compute `overall_score`, `should_apply`, `priority_level`, or `safety_tier`. n8n computes those deterministically.
